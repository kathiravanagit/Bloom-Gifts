Our story

Started in a small shop, still run the same way

G_giftrees began as a single flower counter with a lavender-painted door. Years on, we still hand-tie every bouquet and pack every hamper ourselves — nothing pre-boxed, nothing mass-produced. If you can dream up a combination, our customizer can probably build it.

We built this shop around one idea: a gift should feel like it was made for exactly one person. That's why almost everything we sell can be resized, restocked, or personalized before it ships.
